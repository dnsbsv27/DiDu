House rules

  • Opening roll — 7 or 11 wins instantly. Snake eyes (2) loses instantly. Any other total sets the point.
  • Point round — the shooter must roll the point again before a 7 appears.
  • Side bets — once a point is live, either player may propose a side bet. The other player has 5 seconds to accept.
  • Settlement — the winner's ledger rises by the full wager; the loser's falls by the same amount.
