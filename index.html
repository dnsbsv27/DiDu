House rules

  • Opening roll — 7 or 11 wins instantly. Snake eyes (2) loses instantly. Any other total sets the point.
  • Point round — the shooter must roll the point again before a 7 appears.
  • Side bets — once a point is live, either player may propose a side bet. The other player has 5 seconds to accept.
  • Settlement — the winner's ledger rises by the full wager; the loser's falls by the same amount.

Table Rules

  • Opening roll — 7 or 11 wins instantly. Snake eyes (2) loses instantly. Any other total becomes the point.
  • Point round — the shooter keeps rolling; hitting the point wins, a 7 first loses.
  • Side bets — proposed after the point is set. Whichever player proposes stakes it; the other has 5 seconds to accept, or it's withdrawn.
  • Ledger — tracks each player's running total across the whole session, not just one round.
  • Shooter rotation — the loser's opponent shoots next round and opens the new wager.
